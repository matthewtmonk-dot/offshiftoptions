# Product Vision

Off Shift Options is a private trading companion for two users. It exists to help Matt and Eric make disciplined cash-secured-put and wheel decisions together while keeping trade execution in Thinkorswim/Schwab.

The product north star is:

**Scanner + Research + Decision History + Performance + Collaboration**

The app should help us find opportunities faster, understand why a setup does or does not meet our criteria, research tickers efficiently, track the full history of what we actually traded, measure whether the strategy is working, project possible future growth from recorded performance, maintain watchlists and alerts, share useful thinking with each other, stay engaged through positive milestones, and eventually synchronize read-only Schwab account/trade information.

## Product Areas

- **Dashboard:** a calm starting point for current accounts, active campaigns, watchlist movement, alerts, and recent shared activity.
- **Scanner:** criteria-based opportunity discovery with clear PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN explanations and no fake live-data claims.
- **Research:** a ticker workspace that combines company context, scanner criteria, option candidates, personal history, and collaboration notes.
- **Tracker:** manual/demo campaign tracking now; eventual read-only Schwab synchronization later. The central question is whether the whole idea made or lost money.
- **Watchlists / Sharing:** shared and private watch items with notes, comments, and future condition-based alerts.
- **Reports / Performance:** win rate, average/median results, duration, return on capital, ticker/profile performance, drawdowns, and account growth.
- **Alerts:** useful reminders when watch conditions, scanner scores, expirations, or campaign states need attention.
- **Achievements:** positive milestones that reward process, recovery, collaboration, and long-term progress without encouraging reckless frequency or risk.

## Principles

- Keep the experience clean, simple, and mobile-first.
- Show the important information first; reveal detail on expand, click, dialog, or drawer.
- Prefer progressive disclosure over giant dashboards, giant forms, or 25 metrics at once.
- Make financial data understandable without hiding the math.
- Label demo/manual/mock data clearly until real read-only sources replace it.
- Enforce privacy server-side. Shared means intentionally shared; private means private.
- Never place trades, route orders, cancel orders, or automate brokerage execution.

## Non-Goals

This is not a trading course website, public social network, video/content platform, brokerage/execution platform, TradingView replacement, or automated trading system.

Thinkorswim/Schwab remain the execution platforms for the foreseeable future. Future Schwab work is read-only: account values, positions, orders, transactions, quotes, and option chains for observation and synchronization only.

## Future Direction

Research should eventually answer: "Is this something we should investigate or trade, based on our criteria and our history?" It should include ticker search, company context, earnings, 52-week range, volume, RSI, Bollinger position, scanner score, criteria explanations, option candidates, previous campaigns, notes, and shared comments. It should not try to recreate a full charting terminal.

Watchlists should grow beyond bookmarks. A watch item can eventually carry conditions such as RSI below a threshold, setup score above a threshold, RoR above a target, strike premium above a target, or earnings far enough away. Shared watch items should use the existing lightweight collaboration model rather than becoming another full social app.

Reports should eventually include projections based on actual historical performance. Projections must be clearly labeled as estimates based on assumptions, never guarantees. Prefer median results, trailing periods, and conservative/historical/strong scenario ranges over one unrealistic straight-line promise.

Achievements should be fun but never shame losses or reward risky behavior. Good categories include firsts, recovery, process, collaboration, experience, profit milestones, and account milestones from roughly $10,250 through long-term seven-figure goals. Private activity must never generate a shared achievement that reveals it.
